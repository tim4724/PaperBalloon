Privacy Policy

In short: To play on paperballoon.games and in our apps you don't need an account with us; we use no cookies, trackers or advertising. During a multiplayer session our game server processes your IP address; your player name and inputs go to the other players, and for direct connections your IP address too. Beyond the session we store no game data. Playing via the third-party platform AirConsole is also subject to its terms, and advertising may appear there (see below).

This privacy policy applies to the Paper Balloon offering: the paperballoon.games website, the web games provided through it (currently HexStacker Party at hexstacker.com and Tiny Track Party at tinytrack.paperballoon.games), the Paper Balloon app (Android and iOS) and the associated TV apps (Android TV and Apple TV). It also covers future Paper Balloon titles insofar as they use the same infrastructure (in particular the game and helper servers described below) – regardless of the domain or subdomain under which they are provided. Playing a game via a third-party platform is described separately under "Use via AirConsole".

Data
IP addresses are processed by the web server when you access our websites and when the apps load the game list, images, trailers or game files from it. Web server access logs are stored for security and operational purposes for a maximum of 7 days and then automatically deleted.
During a multiplayer session, our game server processes your IP address and merely relays the data required for gameplay (e.g. player names and inputs) between the devices; it is not stored beyond the session. When you join via the Paper Balloon app, the room code – scanned, entered, or found on the local network – is transmitted to the game server in order to find the corresponding game room. To reduce input latency, individual inputs may, where technically possible, be transmitted directly between the participating devices; the devices exchange their IP addresses for this purpose. The game server itself does not store any connection data; to the extent that infrastructure-side logs are generated at our processor for technical reasons, the information under "Hosting" applies.
For direct connections between game devices, a helper server (STUN server, see "Hosting") is additionally involved, which helps the devices establish the connection. No game data is transmitted via it, and it stores no data.
The TV apps open a game room and connect to the game server for this purpose, and – for direct controller connections – also to the helper server. As with any device, their IP address is processed in doing so. They do not need to access a website for this; if the app lacks individual game files, it downloads them from our web server.
Besides the controller, the recipient of the connection data is the processor named under "Hosting", who may in turn engage sub-processors; these are contractually bound to the same level of data protection (Art. 28(4) GDPR). The other participants in the same game room receive player names and inputs and, for direct connections, your IP address. No further disclosure takes place. The processing is carried out on the basis of the legitimate interest in operating the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Providing your IP address is technically necessary in order to access our websites and take part in the game; without this data, use of the service is not possible. There is no further statutory or contractual obligation to provide it.
Tracking and local storage
No cookies, no analytics or advertising services and no third-party trackers are used on our websites and in our apps. Only a few settings, game progress and technical caches are stored locally in the device's browser (e.g. player name, player colour, mute state, language and colour scheme, plus a reconnection ID per game room) to retain them for the next session. This local storage is strictly necessary to provide the service you explicitly requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG) and does not serve tracking purposes.
The Paper Balloon app displays the game controller in an integrated browser (WebView); the browser storage described above therefore also applies in the app. In addition, the app itself stores the last used player name on the device, not on our servers, in order to suggest it at the next start; if device backup with Google or Apple is enabled, it may be included in it. The last joined game room (room code and link) is not stored permanently. Where the respective game provides for it, the TV apps store only settings, game progress and technical data (e.g. graphics caches, reconnection data) locally on the device, not on our servers.
Camera and QR scan (App)
The Paper Balloon app can use your device's camera to scan the QR code of a game room shown on the TV or screen. Camera access occurs solely at your active initiative, when you tap the scan function; before first access, the operating system asks for your permission, which you can revoke at any time in the device settings. The camera image is processed entirely locally on the device; only the room link contained in the QR code is evaluated. No images are stored or transmitted to third parties, so no personal data is collected in the process.
Room discovery on the local network (Apps)
An opened game room can be announced on the local network (Wi-Fi) by the devices taking part, so that the Paper Balloon app can find it and you can join without scanning the QR code. The announcement uses the standard mDNS/DNS-SD service ("Bonjour") and as its content holds only the room code; in addition there is a label: for TVs the name set in the device settings, for the app a neutral identifier. For technical reasons the operating system may also send the device's network name. It is intended for the local network; it is not transmitted to us or to third parties. Within that network the announcement is readable by any device; anyone who reads it can join the game room without having seen the room code on the screen.
The Paper Balloon app searches for such announcements while it is open, once you have allowed local network access. You can enable the search in the app; on Android 17 and later the operating system also asks for this access when you first join a game room, because direct connections need it too, and this permission also activates the search. Granted permissions can be withdrawn in the device settings; on older Android versions without such a permission, by clearing the app's data. For each room code found on the network, the app asks the game server whether the room still exists and has free slots; it is only displayed if that lookup confirms it. This involves processing the room code and – as with any connection – your IP address (see "Data"). If the search is active and you are taking part in a game room, the app may in turn announce that room on the local network so that further players can find it. QR scan and room-code entry remain available regardless.
App Stores
The Paper Balloon app and the TV apps are distributed via the Apple App Store and Google Play. When downloading and using them, these platforms process their own data (e.g. download data and – depending on your operating-system settings – diagnostic and crash data) on their own responsibility in accordance with their respective privacy policies. The apps themselves contain no analytics, advertising or tracking services, no crash reporting of their own to third parties and no device or advertising identifiers.

When a game is played via AirConsole, the game connection does not run via our infrastructure but via AirConsole's platform. In this respect AirConsole is an independent controller and processes the data arising from use of the platform in accordance with its own privacy policy (such as profile or account data including the name chosen there, connection data, usage statistics and, where applicable, advertising delivered via the AirConsole interface). For Switzerland, an adequacy decision of the EU Commission is in place. AirConsole provides the game files in this case; we ourselves receive no personal data, and no account with us is required. The game may show advertising delivered by AirConsole there.
Hosting
The websites and the helper servers (including stun.hexstacker.com and stun.paperballoon.games) are hosted on the controller's servers in Germany. The game servers used for multiplayer and room discovery (including ws.hexstacker.com and ws.paperballoon.games) are operated for us by Fly.io, Inc. (Chicago, USA) as a processor pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR. The log output of our game server application contains no IP addresses; Fly.io currently retains these logs for 7 days. According to Fly.io, client IP addresses of inbound connections are not retained in its log store. For the platform's separate security monitoring, Fly.io states longer retention periods without a fixed limit; Fly.io does not state whether this includes players' IP addresses. Upon termination of the processing relationship, the data processed on our behalf is deleted in accordance with the data processing agreement. Transfers to the USA are based on the adequacy decision (EU) 2023/1795, as Fly.io, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Because the game servers run across multiple regions, data traffic may also be routed via other locations outside the EEA; insofar as the adequacy decision does not cover a transfer, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision (EU) 2021/914, Module 2 pursuant to Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR) apply. Connections to the game server are encrypted using TLS. We provide a copy of the Standard Contractual Clauses on request.
